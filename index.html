SISTER Web App

Telkomsel RoaMAX Hajj Quota System - Roaming Package Point Of Sales

01

Urgency

RoaMAX Hajj Internet Package sales at the Surabaya Embarkation Point faced operational bottlenecks due to internal-only system access, which forced booth staff into manual data entry. Given the pilgrims’ brief 24-hour transit window, this inefficient workflow led to severe delays and lost sales opportunities. To resolve this, I developed the SISTER Web App, which successfully streamlined the checkout process by introducing self-service transactions (QR/OTP), a travel agent bulk order portal, and automated payment verification.

02

My Role

⚙️ Full-Stack Developer

Built the entire backend architecture with Laravel, including role-based authentication and authorization system (RBAC).

🗄️ Database Architect

Designed a MySQL database schema with efficient table relationships for user management and transaction history.

🎨 UI Developer

Implemented the entire system interface with Blade templates and responsive custom CSS.

03

Design Process

1
Requirements Gathering

Interviewed Telkomsel stakeholders to understand the requirements and the current workflow.

2
Database Design & ERD

Created an Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) to map the relationships between User, Product, and Transaction entities.

3
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Implemented multi-role: Admin (manage all data), Sales (Create Transaction, Update Status), Travel Agent (Bulking Order), and Customer (Online Booking).

4
Development & Testing

Iterative development with Laravel 10, Eloquent ORM, and feature testing using Apache JMeter for load testing.

UI/UX DESIGN  ·  Figma  ·  Prototyping

Esemka Laundry Project

Web Design & Prototyping Platform Laundry with Figma

01

Urgency

Many laundry customers have trouble tracking the status of their laundry in real time. Most of them have to call or make multiple trips to the store just to check whether their clothes are done or not. Since there are no automatic notifications, clear time estimates, or order history, everything relies heavily on manual reminders from staff. Therefore, this prototype was created to facilitate a simpler tracking process and is ready to move on to the development phase.

02

My Roles

🔍 UX Researcher

Conduct user interviews with 5 laundry customers to understand their needs and frustrations

✏️ UI/UX Designer

Design wireframes, user flows, and high-fidelity prototypes using Figma from scratch.

🧪 Usability Tester

Conduct usability testing sessions with interactive prototypes and refine the design based on feedback.

03

Design Process

1
User Research & Empathy Map

Create an empathy map based on the interview results to identify what users feel, think, and need.

2
User Flow & Information Architecture

Mapping the user journey from onboarding, laundry orders, status tracking, to transaction history.

3
Wireframing (Lo-fi → Hi-fi)

The process evolved from paper sketches to digital wireframes, and then to a high-fidelity design featuring a fresh blue color scheme that conveys cleanliness.

4
Prototype & Usability Testing

An interactive prototype in Figma was tested with three users; the results were used to revise the design before handoff.

Business  ·  SUPPLY CHAIN  · ENTREPRENEURSHIP

ScentStep - Shoe Care

Building a scented shoe care brand from scratch — from a conceptual student project into a market validated product

@scentstep

01

Urgency

During the rainy season in Surabaya, college students and urban residents face persistent difficulties in maintaining clean and odor-free footwear. While demand for care solutions is high, the local market is heavily dominated by generic brands that lack strong visual identities and clear positioning, making it hard to build consumer trust. To address this, I developed ScentStep, a comprehensive shoe care brand and laundry service. By combining premium cleaning with long-lasting fragrances, ScentStep offers an affordable, modern solution tailored to the budget of university students, effectively turning everyday shoe maintenance into an engaging and accessible experience.

02

My Roles

📦 Business Owner

Make real sales, manage a simple supply chain, and learn directly from the market.

💡 Brand Strategist

Determining the positioning, brand value, target market, and unique selling proposition that set ScentStep apart from its competitors.

🎨 Visual Designer

Designing logos, color palettes, typography, and all visual brand assets using Figma and Canva.

03

Design Process

1
Market Research & Competitor Analysis

Analyzed local shoe care brands in Surabaya and identified student pain points during the rainy season to establish a unique positioning focused on anti-bacterial fragrance.

2
Brand Identity & Logo Design

Designed the ScentStep logo featuring a dynamic footwear motif and selected a fresh, vibrant color palette to represent cleanliness and a modern, student-friendly identity.

3
Product Launch & Go-to-Market Day

Executed direct sales and physical branding at the Trans Icon Mall Market Day event, successfully validating the product market fit by selling out all bundled packages.

4
Service Expansion & Digital Marketing

Scaled the business by expanding into regular shoe laundry services for the broader Surabaya market and curated a consistent Instagram grid layout to drive digital engagement.
